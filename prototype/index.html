      Try it on the phone
      • Tap an iconOpens the app. The bar at the bottom takes you home.
      • Slide the A–ZDrag along the letters on the right edge to jump through every app.
      • Swipe a previewWith app names shown, the latest message appears beside the icon. Swipe it sideways to dismiss it.
      • Tap a folderTravel and Work slide open as a row of icons beside the folder. Tap it again, or empty space, to close it.
      • Hold an iconRename it, hide it, put it in a folder or a new one, or add or remove it from favourites.
      • Hold a folderRename it, ungroup it, or remove it from favourites.
      • Swipe upSearch apps. Press Enter to open the top result.
      • Swipe downOpens the notification shade.
      • Double-tapDouble-tap empty space to turn the screen off with Power Bubble.

      Building it

      How each piece maps to Android

      FeatureAndroid
      App list, A–ZLauncherApps.getActivityList() plus a callback for installs and removals. Compose LazyColumn with a custom drag strip.
      Message previewsA NotificationListenerService that you grant notification access. Swiping a preview calls cancelNotification(key).
      WidgetsAppWidgetHost. Adding one goes through the system widget picker or bindAppWidgetIdIfAllowed.
      Double-tapSends Power Bubble's screen-off intent, so the launcher needs no accessibility service of its own.
      Swipe downOpens the real notification shade, via the status bar service or Power Bubble's GLOBAL_ACTION_NOTIFICATIONS.
      IconsLauncherActivityInfo.getIcon(). Themed icons use the app's monochrome icon layer (Android 13+), tinted with wallpaper colours. Apps without one fall back to their normal icon.
      FoldersOnly the launcher knows about them. Each folder is a saved name plus a list of apps, in DataStore or Room.
      Hide, renameStored by the launcher itself (DataStore or Room). Hidden apps stay installed and searchable.
      Wallpaper coloursWallpaperManager.getWallpaperColors() gives the main colours and a "supports dark text" hint. This page does the same analysis in the browser.
      Decided
      • Icons in a column centred on the left, names optional. A new message shows as a dot.
      • Letting go of the A–Z strip over a name opens that app, like Niagara.
      • Hidden apps turn up in search once you type two letters.
      • Tapping the clock opens the Clock app. No weather.
